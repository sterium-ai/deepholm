# ADR NNN: Short decision title

> **In short:** one or two plain-language sentences.

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded | rejected
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Scope:** product | simulation | content | presentation | persistence
- **Implements / Depends on / Supersedes / Extends:** optional; related ADRs or documents

## Context

What problem or contract ambiguity requires a decision? Link to the affected
canonical document and cite evidence when the question came from design
research or playtest notes.

## Decision

State the chosen behavior and the boundary it creates. Include stable IDs,
versioning, determinism, and migration consequences where relevant.

## Alternatives considered

List the credible alternatives and why they were not selected.

## Consequences

Describe benefits, costs, risks, deferred work, and any ownership changes.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The affected canonical document is updated.
- [ ] Contracts, examples, and tests are updated or explicitly marked N/A.
- [ ] Save/migration impact is handled.
- [ ] The source-of-truth index still points to the authoritative location.
