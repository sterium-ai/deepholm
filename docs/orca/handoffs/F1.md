## CI fix after the rebase on L1 (PR #46)

CI failed the warnings gate on the rebased head: `check_core_warnings: FAIL, warnings rose:
unsafe_method_access` (228 against the baseline's 227). The one new warning was mine:
`farm_system.gd:117`, `FarmSystem.reconcile_all()` called `TileGrid.tile_at(index)`, which L1
(ADR 045 §2) replaced with `coord_of(index)`. So it was more than a warning: the call names a
method `TileGrid` no longer has, and `reconcile_all()` runs on every `decode()` and on a
truncated dirty-cell stream. Fixed by typing to L1's API, without touching the baseline:

- `farm_system.gd`: `reconcile_all()` uses `_tile_grid.coord_of(index)`; `_set_tile()` uses
  `_tile_grid.index_of(cell)`; the `crops` key is `TileGrid.comma_key(x, y)` ("x,y", the format
  L1 gives one owner; byte-identical, so `state_hash()` and saves are unchanged).
- `validators/farm_validator.gd`: its duplicate-target key is `TileGrid.comma_key(x, y)` too.
- No other F1 code computes a flat index, a tile key or a distance by hand (checked over the
  branch diff: the remaining `"%d,%d"` was the validator's, now replaced).

## What changed

- **Crops are content.** New `game/content/crops.json` + `schemas/crops.schema.json` (an optional
  collection kind in `ContentRegistry`, cross-checked by `core/farming/crop_content.gd`: stages,
  withered tile, seed item and jobs must exist, and the jobs must do the crop's work; a window
  missing from the calendar means no season).
  One crop, `carrot`: seed `seed`, jobs `sow`/`harvest`, window `sow`
  (days 1-20), `grow_days` 4, stages `planted` → `crop_grown` → `crop_ripe`,
  `wither_days_after_window` 1, `withered_tile` `plowed_soil`. New tiles `crop_grown`,
  `crop_ripe` (passable, like `planted`); new item `food` (kind `food`); new job `harvest`.
- **Stages are tile kinds.** `core/farming/farm_system.gd` (`FarmSystem`) keeps one growth record
  per sown tile, `crops["x,y"] = {crop, growth}`. Growth is +1 per tick while the crop's window is
  open; stage i shows from `growth >= ripe * i / (n - 1)` with `ripe = 4 * 2200` ticks (so grown
  at 2 days, ripe at 4). From the start of day `to + 1 + 1` (day 22) an unripe crop's tile turns
  back into `plowed_soil` and the record goes; a ripe crop never withers. No RNG: tick-based.
- **Records follow the tiles.** `FarmSystem.advance()` reads the tile grid's dirty-cell stream
  (A6) and reconciles the changed cells (stage tile ⇒ record at its stage's threshold; anything
  else ⇒ no record). A truncated stream and `decode()` reconcile the whole map. New
  `TickPipeline` phase `farming`, after `dig_finds` and before `refused_reservations`, so a sow or
  harvest completed in a tick has its record before the tick ends.
- **Harvest is a plain `jobs.json` kind** (ADR 051 effects, no code): labour `farm`,
  `reserve, go_to, work, release_all`, 30 work ticks, `requires_tile: crop_ripe`,
  `set_tile: plowed_soil`, `yields: {placement: target, always: [food, food, seed]}`. The items
  land on the tile and `HaulGiver` carries them to a stockpile like any loose item.
- **FarmGiver** (`core/jobs/givers/farm_giver.gd`), built and run by `FarmSystem` after growth:
  the ripe crop's `harvest_job` on every ripe tile (zone or per-tile sow), then per farm zone (id
  order, row-major) a `till` on passable soil and, only while the crop's window is open, the zone
  crop's `sow_job` on plowed soil, never more sows than ground units of that crop's seed not
  already promised to a live sow. Every live till/crop job (the player's per-tile orders
  included) claims its tile. Priority is the player's default (`JobQueue.Priority.NORMAL`).
- **Farm zones.** `zone_add` takes optional `kind` (`"stockpile"` default | `"farm"`) and `crop`
  (required for farm, refused on a stockpile; `invalid_payload`/`unknown_crop` when the crop is
  not in content). `ZoneTable` adds `{"kind": "farm", "crop"}` to farm zones only, so stockpile
  records (and their hash) are unchanged. A farm zone stores nothing: `is_in_any_zone()` and the
  new `get_stockpile_zones()` skip it; `WorldState` now hands `get_stockpile_zones` (not
  `get_zones`) to `HaulGiver` and the tool-drop toil. Overlap is still checked across all zones.
- **Save schema v6.** Zones may carry `kind`/`crop`; new top-level `crops`
  (`[{target, crop, growth}]`, validated by `validators/farm_validator.gd` against the session's
  crop ids; `object_validator.gd` accepts a farm zone with a known crop). `SaveMigrations` v5 → v6
  adds `crops: []`. Fixture `game/scripts/tests/fixtures/save_schema_v6_fixture.json`, written by
  `docs/orca/handoffs/F1-write-save.gd` (a copy of `A7-write-save.gd`, the R14/A5/A13b/A16b/A7
  session): `hash=1180613310`, the same hash A5, A13b and A7 report for that session, so a world
  without crops hashes as before (`hash_into()` adds `crops` only when non-empty). It differs
  from A7's v5 fixture only in `schemaVersion` and `crops: []`; the v1 to v5 fixtures migrate to
  it exactly, and the v5 → v6 step alone turns the (unpacked) v5 fixture into it (tests).
- **Scenario calendars (A16b).** A scenario may replace `calendar.json` (ADR 057). A crop whose
  window that calendar lacks gets no season: it never grows, a zone never sows it, and an
  unripe one withers at once (no crash on a missing window).
- **Viewer.** `crop_grown`/`crop_ripe` draw the planted ground (`SOURCE_FLOORS (1,6)`) with decor
  cells (1,0) and (2,0) of the sprout source. Pack art: the Pixel Crawler pack has crops, so
  the B3 measuring script maps `Farm.png`'s carrot row (the row the sprout already came from):
  grown `[68,20,9,12]`, ripe `[82,16,10,16]` (measured, not typed). The sprout source's cell grew
  from 7x8 to 10x16 to fit them. Placeholder: `tools/generate_placeholder_art.py` now draws
  `sprout.png` as three cells and `build_terrain_tileset.gd` registers them (`.tres`
  regenerated by the builder: +2 cells only). Flat-colour mode colours for both tiles. A
  "Farm Zone" toolbar tool paints a farm zone of the first crop (`OrderInput.Tool.FARM_ZONE`,
  `MapView.set_zone_payload()`); farm zone cells are no longer drawn as stockpile counts. Texts
  `controls.tool_farm_zone`, `tile.crop_grown`, `tile.crop_ripe`, `crop.carrot`,
  `status.job.harvest`, `rejection.unknown_crop` (`test_text_pseudo_locale` requires a text per
  job kind and per core rejection detail); `rejection.zone_add_payload` mentions the farm fields.

## Known limitations

- Colonists do not eat `food` yet: `eat_food` still targets ground berries (`NeedsSystem`, not
  owned). Stored food is inert until a follow-up makes it edible.
- The window is the calendar's day range (`from`/`to`, which F0 also derives for a month-declared
  window) on the absolute day number, as F0's own queries read it. Windows do not repeat yearly
  (F0 keeps them absolute too), so after day 20 of year 1 nothing grows until a later calendar
  change makes windows recur.
- A per-tile sow outside the window is still accepted (unchanged command rules): that crop never
  grows and withers on the wither day.
- `FarmGiver` walks each farm zone's cells every tick; cost grows with zone area (fine for v1
  zones, worth a dirty-driven pass for very large zones).
- Only the first crop is offered by the "Farm Zone" tool (one crop in v1; a crop picker is UI
  beyond painting, a non-goal). The per-tile `sow` order always sows the carrot (its command has
  no crop field); a second crop is sown by farm zones.
- `is_in_any_zone()` now means "in a stockpile": besides hauling and rooms (intended),
  `WorldState._is_bare_tile()` (where an incident may target) therefore treats an empty farm
  zone tile (growing crop or not) as bare, as it treats any other ground with nothing on it.
- A load with a starving colonist and no need source does not continue exactly (pre-F1
  `NeedGiver` persistence gap, see "Review round 2"); F1's farm state itself continues exactly.
- The viewer has no zone outline for either zone kind (unchanged); a farm zone shows through its
  tilled and growing tiles. Owner playtest of the art and the tool is pending (post-merge).
