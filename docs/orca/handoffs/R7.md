# R7 handoff: extract `TradeSystem`

> **In short:** `game/scripts/core/economy/trade_system.gd` (`TradeSystem`) now owns the
> pending trade offers, posting a trader's offer and the `accept_trade` command. `WorldState`
> delegates and keeps `get_pending_trade_offers()` as a one-line delegation (public surface).
> Behaviour is unchanged. A trade-heavy scenario (19 traders, accepted, blocked and refused
> offers, save/load with offers in flight) produces the same saves, events, per-tick trace and
> `state_hash()`, byte for byte, as `main`. Against current `main` (R6 merged), `world_state.gd`
> drops from 2995 to 2871 lines.

**Move, not rewrite.** [`R7-move_check.py`](R7-move_check.py) takes each of the 7 functions from
`main`'s `world_state.gd` and applies the listed mechanical renames. It then compares the result
with the body in `trade_system.gd`, statement by statement, with comment and blank lines
dropped. The renames are:

- the new names;
- `_pending_trade_offers` → `pending_offers`;
- `PRIORITY_TICK` → `_priority_tick` (the injected value of `WorldState.PRIORITY_TICK`);
- `is_tool_kind`, `_find_colonist`, `_find_job_colonist`, `_finish_job` and
  `_remove_colonist_by_id` → injected `Callable`s (`.call(`). The two `var x := …` lines that
  assigned a `Callable` result get an explicit type (`var actor: Dictionary =`,
  `var trader: Dictionary =`), because GDScript cannot infer a type from `Callable.call()`.

Result: `move_check: 7 function(s), 0 differ`.

**Dependencies (no back-reference).** `TradeSystem.new(clock, content, item_table, scheduler,
event_log, priority_tick, is_tool_kind, find_colonist, find_job_colonist, finish_job,
remove_colonist_by_id)` is built in `WorldState._init()` right after `TrenchSystem`. Its
arguments are:

- the `SeededClock`, `ContentRegistry`, `ItemTable`, `GlobalAssignment` and `EventLog`
  instances, which `StateCodec.decode()` restores in place and never reassigns;
- the `PRIORITY_TICK` constant;
- five bound `WorldState` methods as `Callable`s.

It never stores or receives `WorldState`.

**Kept in `WorldState` on purpose** (receiver renames only):

- the `"accept_trade"` arm of `apply()` (R10 owns the router);
- the call in `_set_work_progress()` that posts an offer on an incident job's work tick (R4/R11 code);
- the offer cleanup in `_finish_job()`, the shared terminal boundary.

## Contract impact

- No schema bump, migration, ADR or contract change. `state_codec.gd` is unchanged.
- **Save format:** unchanged. Pending offers were never persisted (ADR 035) and still are not,
  so `TradeSystem` has no `encode()`/`decode()`. The class doc says so, and there is nothing for
  the codec to delegate. The trader's `inventory` component is part of the actor record, which
  the codec already writes. The 6 `save_*.json` and `state_*.json` files below are byte-identical.
- **`state_hash()` coverage:** unchanged. `TradeSystem` has no `hash_into()`.
  - Included (unchanged): the trader's `inventory` (part of `colonists`), the swapped stock items
    (`items`), and the `trade_offer` / `command_applied` events through the event log.
  - Deliberately excluded (unchanged): `pending_offers`. The probe shows it on both sides. At 22
    points with an offer in flight, the live world and its save/load copy have different pending
    offers (`[...]` vs `[]`) but the same `state_hash()`.
- **Invariants from trimmed comments** are in the `TradeSystem` class doc:
  - an offer is posted once, on the trader's first work tick; `pending_offers` is the guard;
  - the pick is deterministic from the trader id's hash over the sorted non-tool kinds, with no
    global randomness and no RNG stream;
  - `accept_trade` takes only an unreserved stockpiled unit, never an item a colonist is holding;
  - an offer never outlives its trader (`_finish_job()` erases it);
  - offers are neither persisted nor hashed.

  Each moved function keeps a 1–3 line *why*.

### Byte identity

[`R7-scenario_dump.gd`](R7-scenario_dump.gd) uses seed 777, a 48x48 map, incidents on and a 6x6
stockpile zone. Every 90 ticks the zone is restocked with one unit of every non-tool item kind,
and a `trader_visit` is spawned every 45 ticks. Each new offer is handled in rotation:

- accepted at once;
- accepted after every stock unit of its `want_item` was removed (`blocked_missing_input`), then
  restocked and accepted again;
- refused (left to despawn on its own wait).

Every 70 ticks the probe also sends malformed `accept_trade` commands (no `trader_id`, a
non-string one, an unknown one, and a `preview()`). At the end of each 150-tick leg it sends a
late `accept_trade` for every trader ever seen. Every 15 ticks with an offer pending, the world is
saved and reloaded into a side copy. Every 150 ticks it is saved raw and through `SaveIO`,
reloaded, and re-saved. Play continues on the original world, because a reloaded world has
incidents off (existing behaviour of `StateCodec.decode()`, not touched here). Every tick the
probe traces the pending offers, every actor's inventory, the item list and `state_hash()`.

```sh

mkdir -p "$S/base" "$S/before" "$S/after"
git archive 279b42f game tools docs | tar -x -C "$S/base" && "$G" --headless --path "$S/base/game" --import

for f in "$S"/before/*; do cmp "$f" "$S/after/$(basename "$f")" && echo "$(basename "$f") identical"; done
```
