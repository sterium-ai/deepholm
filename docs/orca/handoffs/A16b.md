# A16b handoff: scenario roster, calendar and incident list

> **In short:** A scenario entry can now set its starting roster (a colonist count, plus labour
> levels for some colonists), its own calendar, and the list of incidents that can happen.
> `ScenarioBuilder` passes them to `WorldState` at construction through a new `WorldSetup`
> module. After review round 1 the calendar and the incident list are also **saved** (save
> schema **v3**; it becomes **v4** once A13b's own v2 → v3 lands first), so a loaded game and a
> replay keep them. A roster that does not fit the spawn clearing is now rejected instead of
> silently truncated. `world_state.gd` went from 790 to 783 lines by moving two blocks out
> unchanged. The shipped `new_game` and `debug` scenarios use none of the new fields: their
> event logs and 100 ticks of hashes are identical to `main`, and their saves differ only by
> the schema version and the base-start `scenarioStart`. ADR 057 records the decision.

alone. Growing the rectangle would change `world_generator.gd`, which sits at its frozen size
exception (867 of 870 lines). The build-time check stays as a guard for any fallback that comes
up short; no map in that probe reached it, so it has no direct test.

## Contract impact

- `scenarios.schema.json`: three new **optional** fields; existing entries stay valid unchanged.
- New registry rules, all typed and naming `scenarios.json` (ADR 057, "Decision"), including the
  roster limit.
- `WorldState._init()` gains a 7th optional parameter; every existing caller is unchanged.
- **Save format: schema bump v2 → v3** (v4 after A13b), with migration and fixture:
  - new required top-level field `scenarioStart`: `{"calendar": <calendar document> | null,
    "enabledIncidents": [<incident id>, ...]}`;
  - `null` means `calendar.json` and `[]` means every incident, so a base-start world saves
    `{"calendar": null, "enabledIncidents": []}`;
  - the v2 → v3 migration adds exactly that, which is what a v2 world always ran on;
  - not in `state_hash()`, like the content files it replaces.
- ADR 057 (the number the owner assigned: 054 A8, 055 A9, 056 A13b).

## What moved out of `world_state.gd` (same statements)

- `WorldSetup.startup_tables()`: the tool-requirements check, then the haul-entry check, the same
  messages and order. The one difference is that the haul entry is looked up before the tool
  error is tested (a pure lookup, no side effect); `_fail_startup()` still fires with the tool
  error first.
- `IncidentScheduler.reconcile_after_load()`: the loop from
  `_reconcile_incident_jobs_after_load()`, statement for statement. `_finish_job(id, "cancel_job")`
  is the scheduler's existing `_retire_job` (`_finish_job.bind("cancel_job")`), and
  `not _find_colonist(id).is_empty()` is passed in as `actor_exists`. Typed locals replace the
  `String(...)` casts (the warnings gate).

## Acceptance

1. **A scenario with 5 colonists, its own calendar windows and one enabled incident builds
   headless with a deterministic `state_hash()`:** `test_scenario_start.gd` builds `five_camp`
   from a pack: a roster of 5 with two labour definitions, a 100-tick-day calendar with two
   windows, and only `trader_visit` enabled. Two builds hash alike at build time and after 40
   ticks, and another seed differs. The test also checks:
   - 5 colonists and 5 beds spawn;
   - the labour definitions land on `colonist_0`/`colonist_1` with the table's key order
     unchanged, and the rest keep defaults;
   - the scenario's boosts apply and `calendar.json`'s do not, on the scenario's day length;
   - over 12 days only `trader_visit` is drawn, where the same scenario without the list draws
     others;
   - 12 bad start fields fail typed, including a roster of 37;
   - a full 36-colonist clearing builds in full, and the base scenarios keep the base start.

   Round 1 adds two persistence checks:
   - **Save/load:** `five_camp` saved at tick 150 through `SaveIO` and loaded back (`decode()`,
     then `enable_incidents()`, as `boot.gd` does) hashes alike. The loaded world keeps the day-2
     quarry boost and not `calendar.json`'s, ticks in step with the original through day 12, and
     draws only `trader_visit`.
   - **Replay:** a recorded `five_camp` session (`ReplayLog`, checkpoints every 50 ticks, passed
     through `to_dict()`/JSON/`from_dict()`) replays to every checkpoint and the final hash.

   Mutation checks:
   - with the `_enabled_ids` test disabled in `_run_daily_draw()` the test fails ("only the
     enabled incident may be drawn, got [wildlife_wander, trader_visit, wolf_attack, …]");
   - with `StateCodec.decode()` ignoring `scenarioStart` the four save/load checks fail, and the
     replay fails with `diverged` at checkpoint tick 50.

## Known limitations

- The schema number collides with A13b's; whichever merges second renumbers (see "Schema
  numbering" above).
- The build-time "placed fewer colonists than the roster" guard has no direct test: no map in a
  15-case probe reached it (the load-time clearing check is what bounds the roster).
- Per-colonist definitions only carry `labour`. Names, traits or starting gear would be new fields.
- The debug `spawn_incident` command bypasses the enabled list, as it bypasses `min_day`,
  cooldowns and the budget.
- `incidents_enabled` stays the master switch and is still not saved (the boot layer re-applies
  it after a load, as before). An empty list is rejected: "no incidents" is
  `incidents_enabled: false`.
