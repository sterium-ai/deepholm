# F2 handoff: workbench recipes, craft labour and item groups

> **In short:** Workbenches now make axes, picks, planks and stone blocks from `recipes.json`.
> Each bench has "keep N in stock" targets and a queue of orders. Haulers bring the inputs
> (`craft_fetch`, the site_fetch mechanism) and a colonist with the `craft` labour makes the
> item (`craft_work`). The output is hauled to a stockpile. Items declare groups. Save schema
> v7 (after F1's v6), ADR 061. `world_state.gd` shrank (786 -> 767 against `main`). Suite green;
> see the line below.

## Review round 1 (`.orca-review.md`, REQUEST_CHANGES) -- what changed

Blocking, all fixed:
1. **Targets counted loose items anywhere and carried units** (`crafting_system.gd`
   `stock_count`), not the design's stockpiled count; an unreachable ground axe suppressed
   "keep 1 axe" forever. Stock is now: loose units on a stockpile tile, tool items stocked in a
   stockpile, plus units a haul is taking to a stockpile right now (a loose item named by a
   queued or active haul job with no block reason, or what a colonist on a haul job carries).
   The haul term is what stops an output waiting for its hauler from being crafted twice; a
   blocked haul (unreachable item, no free cell) does not count. Not a design change: it is the
   "stockpiled count" the design states, with in-flight hauls to a stockpile counted as
   arriving. ADR 061 "Stock" paragraph rewritten. New tests, both through commands and ticks:
   `_check_target_ignores_an_unreachable_loose_axe` (a loose axe walled in by rock; the bench
   still makes and stocks one axe, exactly one axe's inputs spent) and
   `_check_target_does_not_recraft_output_waiting_for_its_hauler` ("keep 2 planks" with 5
   wood: exactly 2 planks made and 1 wood spent, 600 ticks after they are stocked). Checked
   that they discriminate: with the old counting the first fails ("an unreachable loose axe is
   not stock"); counting stockpile tiles only, the second fails ("4 made").
2. **Duplicate recipe inputs** credited every delivery to each matching held entry.
   `ContentRegistry._check_crafting_content()` now rejects an item listed twice in a recipe's
   inputs or outputs (`schema_violation`), and `deposit_into()` credits only the first entry
   (`break`) as a second guard. Test: a content fixture with two `wood x1` inputs is refused.
   With duplicates impossible in loaded content, the reviewer's cancellation scenario cannot be
   reached; the existing cancel/remove-bench test still proves nothing is lost.
3. **Recipe quantities had no cap; the save caps held quantities at 999.** One constant,
   `RecipeBook.MAX_COUNT` (999), now bounds recipe quantities (registry, `schema_violation`;
   `"maximum": 999` in `recipes.schema.json` for readers, since the registry's schema subset
   does not enforce `maximum`), target/order counts (`CraftingSystem.MAX_COUNT`) and saved held
   quantities (`CraftingValidator.MAX_COUNT`). Tests: a recipe quantity of 1000 is refused and
   999 loads; `_check_recipe_and_save_quantity_caps_agree` writes a real save through
   `SaveIO.write_atomic()` with a bill holding 999 units (reads back) and 1000 (refused).

- Replay check: it now asserts the cancellation really happened (`cancelled`) and that the

## Design, line by line

- Recipes as content on the 2x1 `workbench`: `recipes.json`, axe 3 wood + 1 stone, pick 2 wood +
  2 stone, planks 1 wood -> 2, stone_blocks 2 stone -> 1. Planks and blocks are `material` items
  and not used by any build cost.
- Target "keep N in stock" and an order queue, coexisting: `craft_set_target` /
  `craft_add_order` / `craft_cancel_order`. A bench takes orders first (queue order), then
  targets below stock (recipe id order). Stock = units in a stockpile (loose or stocked tools)
  plus units an unblocked haul is taking to one, so a tool taken by a colonist re-triggers its
  target and an output waiting for its hauler is not made twice.
- `craft` labour does the work: `craft_work` (labour `craft`). Inputs are hauled by haulers
  through the site_fetch mechanism: `craft_fetch` (labour `haul`) shares site_fetch's toils,
  activation and item reservation (`FETCH_KINDS`); one craft_fetch per bench at a time.
- Output on a tile next to the bench, hauled like harvest and berries: `complete_craft()`
  places one stack per output on the first free neighbour of the footprint; HaulGiver takes it.
- Item groups in `items.json`: top-level `groups`; an item's existing `kind` is its group
  (checked by the registry). No filter UI.

**Deviations, called out (no owner decision needed, none reopens the design):**
1. `groups` also lists `valuable`: `gold_coin` already had that kind; removing it was outside F2.
2. A crafted tool is a stackable item until it lies in a stockpile, then
   `CraftingSystem.stock_tools()` makes it a `ToolItemStore` tool item there. Tools have no haul
   path of their own; this keeps "appears next to the bench and is hauled to a stockpile".
3. One `craft_work` duration for all recipes (`jobs.json` `work_ticks` 60); per-recipe
   durations need a resume hook like site_work's (ADR 061, alternatives).
4. A bill whose inputs vanish before they are all delivered is abandoned; its held inputs drop
   next to the bench and get hauled back. That is what "waits without blocking the queue"
   means once a bill has started.
5. Output hauling follows the ground-yield pattern (`ItemTable` ground items + HaulGiver),
   the same one F1's harvest uses now that it is on `main` (see "Branch and commit"). No
   follow-up.

## Contract impact

- ADR 061 (new). Save schema **v7** (`crafting`), migration v6 -> v7 after F1's v5 -> v6,
  fixture `save_schema_v7_fixture.json` (same session as v1 to v6, written by

- Commands: three new types (`core/commands/README.md`).
- `state_hash()`: `crafting` is hashed once a bench was configured (`CraftingSystem.hash_into()`),
  so a world without crafting keeps its pre-F2 hash.
- Knock-on effect: `TradeSystem._pick_trade_items()` picks two of the non-tool item ids by
  `hash(trader_id) % count`, so two new items change which goods a trader offers (all trader
  tests pass).
- `manifest.json`: unchanged. It only carries the bundle version, which every committed save
  fixture records, and the registry finds `recipes.json` by its schema. Bumping it would need a
  content-rename entry for every older save.
