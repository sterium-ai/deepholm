# R8 handoff: extract `NeedsSystem`

> **In short:** `game/scripts/core/actors/needs_system.gd` (`NeedsSystem`) now owns need decay
> and the needs/labour backfill, pausing and resuming a colonist's job around a need (or
> combat/rescue) interrupt, the paused-jobs map and its persistence, the exposed need reason
> and the `need_unmet` event, the kind-aware source candidates and the effect of a completed
> need job. `WorldState` delegates to it, and `NeedGiver` is built on its callables instead of
> `WorldState` methods. Behaviour is unchanged. A needs scenario with eight mid-game save/load
> round trips produces the same saves, per-segment event logs (captured before every reload),
> deep-copied per-5-tick trace and `state_hash()`, byte for byte, as `main`, and so do the R1–R6 probes. `world_state.gd` drops from 2995 to 2648 lines.

**Dependencies (no back-reference).** `NeedsSystem.new(content, need_definitions, need_full,
event_priority, clock, calendar, scheduler, toils, progress_table, tool_store, item_table,

after `ToilExecutor` (it needs `_toils`), before `_build_dimension_services()` (which builds
`NeedGiver` and `RescueGiver` from its callables). Its arguments are:

- sibling modules and services that `StateCodec.decode()` restores in place and never
  reassigns (`_clock`, `_calendar`, `_scheduler`, `_toils`, `_progress_table`, `_tool_store`,
  `_item_table`, `_tile_grid`, `_object_table`, `_event_log`, `_trench`), plus `_content`,
  the live `_need_definitions` Dictionary (shared by reference with `NeedGiver`, as before:
  tests mutate a kind's `rate_per_day` in place) and `_need_full`;
- a lambda returning `_colonists`, because `decode()` reassigns that field;
- eight bound `WorldState` methods and `_combat_giver.owns` (built once in `_init()`).

It never stores or receives `WorldState`. Two constructions earlier in `_init()` need it
before it exists, so they get lambdas: `CombatGiver`'s `interrupt_current_job` /
`resume_interrupted_job` and `ToilExecutor`'s `source_valid`
(`func(job_id: String) -> bool: return _needs_system.consume_source_valid(job_id)`).
The construction order of every other object is unchanged.

**`NeedGiver` depends on `NeedsSystem`.** Six of its constructor callables now come from the
module: `need_job_targets`, `need_source_candidates`, `set_need_reason`,
`emit_need_unmet_event`, `interrupt_current_job`, `resume_interrupted_job`. `RescueGiver`'s
interrupt/resume callables do too. `need_giver.gd` changes only in doc comments (they now name
the `NeedsSystem` functions).

**Kept in `WorldState` on purpose:**

- `get_active_need_job_id()`: it is already a one-line delegation to
  `NeedGiver.get_pending_job()`, which owns that state; routing it through `NeedsSystem`
  would add a hop over a giver that `decode()` rebuilds. It stays as public surface.
- `_need_definitions`, `_need_full`, `NEED_FULL`, `_load_need_definitions()`,
  `_load_need_config()`: content configuration, read by `_init()`, `_build_dimension_services()`
  (`NeedGiver`) and by 16 tests (`world._need_definitions[...]`, `world._need_full`). Both
  modules receive the same values.
- The readers of `paused_jobs` that belong to other tasks (`_cancel_haul_job_carrying_consumed_item()`,
  `_drop_carried_haul_item()` — R11; `_cleanup_actor_scheduling()` — R9) and the callers of
  `need_kind_for_job()`/`apply_need_effect()` in the toil hooks (R11): receiver renames only.
- `_advance_go_to_or_resubmit()`, `_colonist_within_reach()`, `_route()`, `_labour_table()`,
  `_default_labour_table()`, `_ensure_health()`, `_ensure_combat()`, `_tile_key()`: used by

### Byte identity

[`R8-scenario_dump.gd`](R8-scenario_dump.gd) uses seed 4242, a 48x48 map and incidents on.
At tick 0 it:

- clears an 11x11 yard at the map centre, moves the 3 spawned colonists there and adds 3
  more, starting them at mixed need levels (some urgent or critical at once);
- multiplies every need's `rate_per_day` by 25 (again after each reload), so every colonist
  goes through several warn/urgent/critical cycles in 800 ticks;
- adds a two-tile pond, three ground-berries tiles and two beds, one inside a walled 3x3
  bedroom (the bedroom rest multiplier);
- orders chop/till/dig/mine work around the yard and puts wood on the ground with a
  stockpile zone, so needs interrupt work toils and hauls mid-carry; wolves arrive at ticks
  150 and 450 (combat interrupts).

Every 20 ticks it issues three random orders, a `cancel_job` on a live need job, or a
`cancel_job` on a paused job. Every command is previewed, then applied. Every 100 ticks the
world is saved (raw and through `SaveIO`), reloaded with `from_save_state()`, and play
continues on the reloaded copy. Every 5 ticks the probe traces every actor's needs,
accumulator, need reason, active need job, work and route state, the raw paused-jobs and
need-reason maps, the need jobs, `NeedGiver`'s pending map, the ground berries and
`state_hash()`. Each trace line is stored as a deep copy (`duplicate(true)`), so later ticks
cannot rewrite earlier observations through the live needs, accumulator, paused-job, reason
or job dictionaries. The event log is runtime only (a reload starts it empty), so the probe
stores a deep copy of it right before every reload: `events.json` holds the full log of each
of the eight 100-tick segments.
