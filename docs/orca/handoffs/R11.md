**Why the four delegations remain.** Each one is a `Callable` already handed to a module
built before `ToilBindings` (WorkProgress, ToilExecutor, ConstructionSystem, TrenchSystem,
CombatSystem) or to R10's handlers. A bound `WorldState` method resolves `_toil_bindings` at
call time, so these constructors and R10's wiring stay exactly as they were, and the files
the owner asked not to touch stay untouched. R12 can inline them when it reorders `_init()`.

**Move, not rewrite.** [`R11-move_check.py`](R11-move_check.py) does three things for each
of the 27 moved functions:

1. Takes the function from the base's `world_state.gd`, signature line included.
2. Applies the listed mechanical renames.
3. Compares the result with the function in `toil_bindings.gd`, statement by statement,
   with full-line comments and blank lines dropped.

No statement is added, dropped or reordered. The renames are:

- the new names (table above);
- `get_tile(` → `_tile_grid.get_tile(`, `get_tool_items()` → `_tool_store.get_items()`,
  `get_tool_item_reservation` → `_tool_store.reservation_of`, `_get_job(` →
  `_scheduler.queue.get_job(` (the `WorldState` methods were one-line delegations to exactly
  these);
- `_finish_job`, `_find_job_colonist`, `_find_colonist`, `_resubmit_unreachable_job`,
  `_routable_to`, `_route_cost` → injected `Callable`s (`.call(`);
- because a `Callable` returns `Variant`, `var x := _f.call(...)` is written with its type
  spelled out (`var x: Dictionary = ...` for the colonist lookups, `var x: int = ...` for
  `_route_cost`), the way NeedsSystem and TrenchSystem already do;
- `_need_giver.resolve_job(` / `_rescue_giver.resolve_job(` →
  `_need_giver_resolve_job.call(` / `_rescue_giver_resolve_job.call(`;
- `_colonists` → `_get_colonists.call()`.

`WorldState._init()` builds it last, right after `CombatSystem`, because its hooks reach

The module never stores or receives `WorldState`, and no other object's construction order

## Contract impact

- No schema bump, migration, ADR or contract change. The entry needs no ADR: no new toil, no
  new decision layer, and the hook names ToilExecutor reads are unchanged.
- **Save format:** unchanged, and `state_codec.gd` is untouched. The module persists
  nothing: route/work and hands live on the colonist records, the reroute searches in
  `WorldState._reroutes`, the site-fetch caches in ConstructionSystem, the tool requirements
  are rebuilt from `jobs.json` at startup, and `hooks` is code. So it has no
  `encode()`/`decode()`, as its class doc states. All `save_*.json` and `states.json` files
  of the six probes are byte-identical (below).
- **`state_hash()` coverage:** unchanged by construction. The module holds no hashed field,
  and `state_hash()` reads none of the moved members (`git diff origin/main` touches no line
  of `state_hash()`). Deliberately excluded before and still excluded: `_toil_hooks`/`hooks`,
  `_tool_requirements_by_kind`, `_reroutes` (runtime search objects). Empirically, every
  probe's `state_hash()` is traced every 5 ticks and after every reload, and all traces are
  identical.
- **Invariants from trimmed comments** are in the `ToilBindings` class doc:
  - the module persists and hashes nothing;
  - cargo is never lost at a terminal transition (the forced-drop fallback);
  - a job paused by a need interrupt deposits its cargo without touching route/work;
  - site_fetch's `cell` is the delivering flag and `site` always names the current
    delivery site, so a mid-chain save/load needs no extra field;
  - incident/flee/approach jobs are cancelled, never resubmitted, on unreachable.

  Each moved function keeps a 1–3 line *why*: the trim before site tiles, the re-check of
  the target tile kind, why the pick-up kind is cached, the "very close" rule, requeue rather
  than suspend.

### Byte identity

[`R11-scenario_dump.gd`](R11-scenario_dump.gd) uses seed 6161, a 48x48 map and incidents
on. At tick 0 it clears a 13x13 soil yard at the map centre, moves the spawned colonists
there and adds three more. It then exercises:

- **Hauls:** stacks of wood, stone, sand and seed around the yard and two stockpile zones.
  The near stockpile is walled off from tick 45 to 200. The far one is removed at tick 140
  (in-flight hauls fail with `blocked_destination_gone` and drop their cargo) and re-added
  at tick 360.
- **Tools:** one axe and one pick for eleven chop/mine/dig orders, so it covers
  `fetch_tool`, `no_tool_found` requeue and the tool gate.
- **Work effects:** chop, mine, dig, till and forage, sow on the tilled tiles at ticks 120
  and 320 (seed runs short), and a second mine on one rock (`invalid_target`).
- **Unreachable targets:** a dig and a chop inside stone-wall rings placed at tick 30 (the
  first leg is unreachable, so the job is resubmitted).
- **Construction:** a workbench, a bed and a three-wall line, so site_fetch picks up, hops,
  deposits and chains.
- **Random play:** every 20 ticks three random orders, a `cancel_job` on a live
  haul/site_fetch, `cancel_job`/`fail_job`/`invalidate_job` on a live work job, or new
  ground stacks.
- **Save/load:** every 100 ticks the world is saved (raw and through `SaveIO`), reloaded
  with `from_save_state()`, and play continues on the reloaded copy. The event log (runtime
  only) is deep-copied right before every reload.

Every 5 ticks the probe traces, each line as a deep copy:

- every actor's position, hands, inventory, held tool and route/work state;
- the reroute keys and every job;
- ground items, and tool items with their reservations;
- the site-fetch caches, the sites, the dirty-cell count and `state_hash()`.
