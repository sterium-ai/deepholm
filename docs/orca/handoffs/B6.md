## What changed and why

Since B2 the body is a 64×64 frame drawn at `tile*16 + SPRITE_POSITION_OFFSET` = tile + (−24, −31),
and the three badges were offset from that frame origin with offsets sized for a 16 px sprite,
so they landed ~28 px left / ~35 px above the tile (`B6-before.png`). Now they are offset from
the tile origin, like the health bar, and keep following the glide (same lerp as the body).

Geometry used (all derived from `TILE_SIZE`, the badge sizes and the frame's measured lines):
the idle/walk/carry silhouette spans frame x 24..40 (= the tile's own 0..16) and its top is frame
y 18 for every one of those animations in `colonist_pack.json` (only the work strips reach higher,
because of the swung tool), so the head top is `HEAD_TOP_Y` = −13 px from the tile origin.

| Badge | Offset from `tile * TILE_SIZE` | Resulting rect (px) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| tool (left of the head) | `(-TOOL_MARKER_SIZE.x, HEAD_TOP_Y)` | x −9.6..0, y −13..−3.4 |
| cargo (right of the head) | `(TILE_SIZE, HEAD_TOP_Y)` | x 16..25.6, y −13..−3.4 |
| trapped (above the head, centred) | `((TILE_SIZE − TRAPPED_MARKER_SIZE.x) / 2, HEAD_TOP_Y − TRAPPED_MARKER_SIZE.y)` | x 1.6..14.4, y −21..−13 |

**Deviation from the entry's wording, called out:** the entry asks for the trapped badge
"above (below the health bar, no overlap)". The health bar (`HEALTH_BAR_OFFSET` = (0, −4.8),
3 px tall, not touched: the entry says it is fine and it is outside this task) actually crosses
the character's face (y −4.8..−1.8, see the captures), so a badge cannot be both above the head
and below the bar. I kept "above the head" and "no overlap"; the test asserts it does not
intersect the bar. If the owner wants the bar moved above the head too, that is a separate
`B*` entry (it changes the health bar, not the badges).

Placeholder path: untouched (non-goal). The placeholder frames share the 64×64 / feet-47 layout,
so the same offsets apply.
