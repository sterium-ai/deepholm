## What changed and why

Since B2 the body is a 64×64 frame drawn at `tile*16 + SPRITE_POSITION_OFFSET` = tile + (−24, −31),
and the three badges were offset from that frame origin with offsets sized for a 16 px sprite,
so they landed ~28 px left / ~35 px above the tile, and the tile-anchored health bar at y −4.8
crossed the face (`B6-before.png`). Now the badges are offset from the tile origin, like the bar,
and keep following the glide (same lerp as the body); the bar moved above the head.

Head geometry (measured, not guessed): every idle/walk/carry alpha bbox in `colonist_pack.json`
starts at frame y 18 (only the work strips reach higher, because of the swung tool), and the
pack's `Idle_Down` frames show the head's widest rows at x 26..37 down to the neck at y 31 (32 on
the bob frame). So `FRAME_HEAD_BOX` = `Rect2(26, 18, 12, 15)`, and `HEAD_BOX` = that box plus
`SPRITE_POSITION_OFFSET` = `Rect2(2, −13, 12, 15)` from the tile origin; `HEAD_TOP_Y` = −13.

| Element | Offset from `tile * TILE_SIZE` | Resulting rect (px) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| head box (reference) | `HEAD_BOX` | x 2..14, y −13..2 |
| health bar (above the head) | `HEALTH_BAR_OFFSET` = `(0, -TILE_SIZE)` | x 0..16, y −16..−13 |
| trapped (centred, directly above the bar) | `((TILE_SIZE − TRAPPED_MARKER_SIZE.x) / 2, HEALTH_BAR_OFFSET.y − TRAPPED_MARKER_SIZE.y)` | x 1.6..14.4, y −24..−16 |
| tool (left of the head) | `(-TOOL_MARKER_SIZE.x, HEAD_TOP_Y)` | x −9.6..0, y −13..−3.4 |
| cargo (right of the head) | `(TILE_SIZE, HEAD_TOP_Y)` | x 16..25.6, y −13..−3.4 |

Adjacent boxes touch edges (bar bottom = head top, trapped bottom = bar top, tool right = bar
left x); `Rect2.intersects()` excludes shared borders, so the test's "no intersection" holds.

The bar is drawn by this file for every actor with health (colonists and also wolves / raiders /
traders), so theirs moves to one tile above their tile too, as the rule says "tile-anchored". No
other actor geometry was in scope.

Placeholder path: untouched (non-goal). The placeholder frames share the 64×64 / feet-47 layout,
so the same offsets apply.
