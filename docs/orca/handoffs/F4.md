# F4 handoff: underground layers — generation, discovery, tunnel, drill and ores

> **In short:** a world starts with its surface only. A finished `tunnel` (cost: a `drill`
> crafted at the workbench + 2 wood) discovers the layer below, generated from the world seed
> alone (`UndergroundGenerator`), so seed 42's L1 is identical whether found at tick 100 or
> 10000. Each finished tunnel carves a 3×3 floor landing below itself with `stairs_up`, which
> pairs with the tunnel's `link_down` through the L3 vertical-link code. Veins (copper/tin/coal
> on L1, iron/silver on L2, gold/gems on L3) are content; mining one yields its ore item (new
> stockpile group `ore`), which colonists haul like any item. Up to L3. The save gains
> `map.undergroundVersion` (schema **v14 locally, to be renumbered v16 on main**). ADR 071
> (proposed). All three packages of the owner note are delivered; nothing is left as F4-3.

## Acceptance → evidence (`test_underground_layers.gd`)

| Acceptance | Check |
|---|---|
| A fresh world holds the surface only | `_check_fresh_world_holds_the_surface_only` |
| Vein ores are `ore` items on their layer | `_check_every_vein_ore_is_an_ore_item_on_its_layer` |
| A tunnel built with a drill creates L1, reachable by stairs | `_check_tunnel_built_with_a_drill_discovers_l1_with_stairs` (real `build` command, a colonist hauls the drill and wood and builds) |
| Seed 42's L1 identical at tick 100 and 10000 | `_check_seed_42_l1_is_the_same_at_tick_100_and_tick_10000` (also: another seed differs) |
| A colonist mines copper and the ore reaches a stockpile | `_check_colonist_mines_copper_and_the_ore_reaches_a_stockpile` (real `mine` + `zone_add` commands; colonist on L1 with a pick) |
| Three tunnels reach L3 with its veins | `_check_three_tunnels_reach_l3_with_its_veins` (and a tunnel on L3 discovers nothing) |
| Save/load continues identically | `_check_save_load_after_discovery_continues_identically` (hash equal after load, after 200 ticks, and after discovering L2 post-load) + the v14 fixture checks in `test_save_migration` |
| Replay reproduces the run | `_check_replayed_run_reproduces_the_discovery` |
| An empty layer doesn't slow the tick | `_check_discovered_empty_layer_does_not_slow_the_tick` |

Honest notes on these checks:
- The three-tunnel, empty-layer and save/load-L2 checks call
  `LayerDiscovery.on_site_completed` directly (the hook `ConstructionSystem` calls) instead of
  building each tunnel, to keep the test short; the L1 checks, the mining check and the replay
  go through real commands and the real build.
- The empty-layer check is a timing bound (deep ≤ 3 × surface + 2 ms/tick over 200 ticks) that
  only catches a per-tile scan of the new layers, not small regressions. By construction an
  undiscovered layer costs nothing: it does not exist in TileGrid until discovered.

## Known limitations

- No tunnel build tool in the viewer's control bar (the tool list is outside F4's paths):
  tunnels are placed by build orders/commands. Follow-up.
- `LayerDiscovery` holds a `weakref` to `WorldState` to reach the grid, object table, event log
  and givers. The "no back-reference to WorldState" rule is for the R* extractions; noted here
  as a deliberate non-R deviation, kept small (one module, one entry point).
- `LayerDiscovery._refresh_giver_bounds` writes the givers' cached `_bounds_max` directly.
- Changing the generator or vein content changes the future layers of existing saves; this is
  what `undergroundVersion` records, but only version 1 exists (ADR 071, Consequences).
- No cave-ins/supports (F4b), smelting, layers below L3, or fog of war (non-goals).
- Placeholder art only.
