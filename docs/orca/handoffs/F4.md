# F4 handoff: underground layers — generation, discovery, tunnel, drill and ores

> **In short:** a world starts with its surface only. A finished `tunnel` (cost: a `drill`
> crafted at the workbench + 2 wood) discovers the layer below, generated from the world seed
> alone (`UndergroundGenerator`), so seed 42's L1 is identical whether found at tick 100 or
> 10000. Each finished tunnel carves a 3×3 floor landing below itself with `stairs_up`, which
> pairs with the tunnel's `link_down` through the L3 vertical-link code. Veins (copper/tin/coal
> on L1, iron/silver on L2, gold/gems on L3) are content; mining one yields its ore item (new
> stockpile group `ore`), which colonists haul like any item. Up to L3. The save gains
> `map.undergroundVersion` (schema **v14 locally, to be renumbered v16 on main**). ADR 071
> (proposed). All three packages of the owner note are delivered; nothing is left as F4-3.

## Review round 1 (`.orca-review.md`) → fixes

Blocking:
1. **No runtime caller of `set_vertical_links`.** `LayerDiscovery.links()` returns the region
   graph's `vertical_links()` on a map with more than one layer. `wire_routing()` gives it to
   the scheduler and the need/rescue/approach givers as `links_of`, and it is called each time
   they are built. `MeasuredRoute.use_links()` passes the links to the `RouteSearch`; the reroute
   and the load's route search use them too. Real cross-layer play then showed two planar
   assumptions, now fixed: assignment scoring (`OTHER_LAYER_DISTANCE` for a target on another
   layer) and the reach / pick-up test in `toil_executor` (false across layers). Evidence: the
   colonist walks down the stairs to build L2's tunnel and to mine on L1 (checks below), with
   0 `SCRIPT ERROR` in the underground test.
2. **Content lint on literals.** `underground_generator.gd` uses `ContentRegistry.TILE_ROCK` /
   `TILE_FLOOR`; `lint_content_literals.ps1` passes (Validation).
3. **Touches outside the owned paths.** Approved by the owner in `6e89e26` (world_state wiring
   only with no growth, construction_system, toil_bindings, tool_matching, tile_grid, lowered
   warnings baseline). world_state is now 767 lines, equal to main. The routing touches are
   declared above as required by decision (2).
4. **More than one tunnel per layer; the landing deleted objects.** `may_place` vetoes a second
   tunnel on a layer (built, a site, or in the same build line) with `tunnel_layer_taken`, and a
   tunnel whose stairs tile below holds a non-vein object with `tunnel_landing_blocked`. The
   landing now only clears rock and veins and keeps every other object.
   `_check_one_tunnel_per_layer`, `_check_landing_blocked_and_landing_keeps_objects`.
5. **Tests bypassed construction; the replay was not a replay.** Every tunnel is now ordered
   with a real `build` command and built by a colonist (materials hauled, the drill consumed).
   The run is recorded with `ReplayLog` and replayed through it, and checkpoints are compared.
   The save/load check saves mid-play between two discoveries, loads, and compares the rest of
   the run against the uninterrupted one.

Non-blocking:
- **Timing tolerance.** `_check_discovered_layers_do_not_slow_the_tick` takes the median of 5
  alternating rounds (surface-only world vs. the same world with L1–L3 discovered) and requires
  the 3-layer tick ≤ 1.5× the surface tick + 0.5 ms. Measured in the final run: 219.4 vs
  265.0 µs/tick (ratio 1.21). Rationale: the tick visits colonists, jobs and dirty cells, never whole layers, so the
  extra layers cost only what their region graph adds. One full pass over three extra layers
  per tick would exceed the tolerance many times over, so the check still catches the
  regression it is for. The alternating median keeps machine noise out.
- **ADR 071 proposed.** Left proposed, with sign-off listed under pending owner approvals.

## Review round 2 (`.orca-review.md`) → fixes

Blocking:
1. **Needs across the stairs.** `NeedsSystem.need_source_candidates` and `_bed_reachable_once`
   filtered sources and beds by planar region, so a colonist on L1 never saw surface food.
   They now filter by `RegionMap.component_of` (the region-graph component, which includes
   the stairs); `_regions_reaching` became `_components_reaching`. Evidence:
   `_check_colonist_on_l1_eats_surface_berries_through_the_stairs`.
2. **Same-layer distance asserts in the givers.** `need_giver`, `rescue_giver` and
   `approach_giver` sorted candidates with `TileGrid.manhattan`, which asserts when the z
   differs. Each now scores a target on another layer at `ZoneTable.OTHER_LAYER_DISTANCE`
   (`NeedGiver._distance()`, and the same rule in the rescue sort and in the approach giver's
   `_best_adjacent_tile` and `_start_search`). `CombatTargeting.nearest_hostile_actor` skips
   hostiles on another layer, so a raider on the surface is no flee threat underground.
   Evidence: `_check_hostile_on_another_layer_is_no_flee_threat`, plus the eat check above,
   which goes through `NeedGiver`.
3. **Content manifest.** The new content files were not listed anywhere that the load checks.
   Mirroring F6b-1 (BACKLOG `e9292ad`): `manifest.json` now lists its 16 content files under
   `content`, the schema allows the array, and `ContentJson.manifest_reference_error()` fails
   the load (`ERROR_MISSING_FILE`) on an unknown or duplicate entry. Evidence:
   `test_content_registry` `_check_shipped_manifest_lists_every_content_file` and
   `_check_fixture_manifest_content_entries_are_checked`.

Non-blocking:

  runs the loaded and the original world in lockstep, compares their hashes on every tick and
  fails at the first mismatch.
- **Warnings.** Round 1's `use_links()` line in `measured_route.gd` added one
  `unsafe_call_argument` (3 against a baseline of 2). Fixed with a typed cast; the baseline is
  unchanged.

## Review round 3 (`.orca-review.md`) → fixes

Blocking:
1. **Resuming work across layers.** `WorldState._colonist_within_reach` called
   `TileGrid.chebyshev()` (which asserts on a z mismatch) when a colonist came back from eating
   on another layer. It is now false across layers, and so are the same-tile/adjacency guards in
   `toil_executor.gd` and `tool_fetch_toil.gd`. Evidence:
   `_check_interrupted_l1_job_resumes_through_the_stairs` (a colonist mining on L1 gets hungry,
   climbs the stairs, eats surface berries, comes back down and finishes the vein).
2. **Admissible bound across layers.** `OTHER_LAYER_DISTANCE` was used as a pruning bound, so
   `_search_can_stop()` could skip a nearer source across the stairs. New
   `TileGrid.route_lower_bound(a, b)` = max(|dx|, |dy|) + |dz|, never more than a real route
   (8-way moves, one step per layer). `NeedGiver._distance()` keeps Manhattan on the same layer
   and uses the bound across layers; `ApproachGiver` uses the bound for its scoring and pruning.
   Evidence: `_check_nearest_food_across_the_stairs_wins` (a diver on L1 at the stairs with a
   berry one step up and another eight steps along an L1 corridor eats the surface one first).
3. **Searches in flight across a discovery.** New `RouteSearch.refresh_topology(links,
   bounds_max)`. `LayerDiscovery.on_site_completed` hands the current links and bounds to every
   search in flight: the reroutes, the scheduler's pending routes and the need, rescue and
   approach givers' searches, which is what `StateCodec` gives them on a load. The combat
   giver's cached bounds are raised too. Evidence:
   `_check_save_load_with_a_search_in_flight_across_discovery`: a probe world finds the
   discovery tick; a second world starts a reroute the tick before, which is still searching on
   the discovery tick and has the new bounds and links (as has every other search in flight);
   the world saved on that tick and the original then tick 300 times in lockstep with equal
   hashes. The round-2 limitation "a route search keeps the links it was created with" is gone
   (ADR 071 updated).
4. **Scope.** The touches outside the owned paths cannot be removed without undoing the fixes
   the reviews asked for. No owner approval for them is recorded in BACKLOG.md (checked on
   `origin/main` after `git fetch`), and none is invented here: they are listed under
   *Pending owner approvals*, with the round-3 additions.
5. **Drill crafted, not injected.** `_check_drill_crafted_at_the_workbench_builds_a_tunnel`:
   a workbench with a `craft_add_order` for `drill`; the colonist hauls wood and stone, crafts
   the drill (wood 4 → 2, stone 3 → 0, drill 0 → 1, counted on the ground, in inventories and
   in bench bills), then builds a tunnel with it and the last 2 wood; L1 is discovered and the
   drill and the wood are used up.

## Known limitations

- No tunnel build tool in the viewer's control bar (the tool list is outside F4's paths):
  tunnels are placed by build orders/commands. Follow-up.
- `LayerDiscovery` holds a `weakref` to `WorldState` to reach the grid, object table, event log
  and givers. The "no back-reference to WorldState" rule is for the R* extractions; noted here
  as a deliberate non-R deviation, kept small (one module, one entry point).
- `LayerDiscovery._refresh_giver_bounds` writes the givers' cached `_bounds_max` directly.
- Changing the generator or vein content changes the future layers of existing saves; this is
  what `undergroundVersion` records, but only version 1 exists (ADR 071, Consequences).
- No cave-ins/supports (F4b), smelting, layers below L3, or fog of war (non-goals).
- Placeholder art only.
- An object built on the stairs tile while the tunnel is still a site means no stairs
  (`may_place` checks only when the tunnel is placed). No deconstruct command exists yet, so a
