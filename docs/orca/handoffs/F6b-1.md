# F6b-1 handoff: wild species, animal actors and population dynamics

> **In short:** six wild species live in `game/content/species.json`, with its schema. Animals
> are records of kind `"animal"`, created by `ActorTable.spawn_animal()` and kept in
> `FaunaSystem`'s own roster. A new TickPipeline phase, `"fauna"`, runs right after
> `"farming"`, surface only, and draws from its own salted RNG stream. Births are logistic
> (`1 − count / cap`). Saves move to schema v14, which adds an optional top-level `fauna`
> field, with a migration and a fixture. The viewer draws the wolf with the B3 pack art and
> the other species as coloured dots. `world_state.gd` stays at 767 lines. With 200 animals
> and 1000 colonists, a tick averages 74.7 ms, against 75.8 ms with no animals. The fauna
> phase alone costs 0.33 ms. Design and decisions: ADR 070.
>
> **Review round 1** (Codex, REQUEST_CHANGES with 5 blocking findings). Three are fixed in
> code with tests: the exact 64-bit `fauna.rng`, species ids checked in saves, and bad species
> content as a fatal typed startup error. The other two are now recorded as owner decisions
> with measured evidence: the separate roster as a contract amendment, and shipped counts as
> a deferral. See *Review response*.

## Contract impact

- Save schema **13 → 14**. The new optional top-level field is
  `fauna: {animals: [...], nextAnimalId, rng: {seed, state}}`. It is shipped with:
  - the migration (`save_migrations.gd`, which adds nothing because an absent field means no
    animals);
  - the validator (`FaunaValidator`);
  - the fixture (`save_schema_v14_fixture.json`);
  - the contract (`game-state.schema.json`).
- `state_hash()`: the fauna block is added by `FaunaSystem.hash_into()` only once an animal has
  ever spawned (`nextAnimalId > 1`). A world with no animals hashes exactly as on main, which
  is why every existing hash and replay test passes unchanged.
- New content kind: `species.json` with its schema. `mapgen.json` gains an optional
  `animal_counts` field; it is absent from shipped content, so the default is no animals.
- View snapshot: a new `animals` list (a deep copy).
- ADR 070 added.

## Acceptance → evidence

Each criterion is exercised through real ticks (`WorldState.tick()`), not internal calls:
1. **20 grasshoppers, no predators, reach the cap and hold it.** `test_fauna_population.gd`:
   the count reaches 60 and stays at 60 over the following window.
2. **20 grasshoppers plus 2 birds stay below the cap, and the birds survive.**
   `test_fauna_population.gd`: the grasshopper peak is under 60, and birds are alive at the
   end.
3. **A wolf with no prey dies within its starvation window.** `test_fauna_population.gd`: the
   wolf is alive at `starve_ticks − 10` and gone at `starve_ticks + 10`.
4. **200 animals with 1000 colonists keep the A14 budget.** `F6b-1-bench.gd`:

   | Run (256×256, seed 42, 1000 colonists, 60 ticks) | Mean | p95 | Max |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | 0 animals | 75.77 ms | 81.54 ms | 100.47 ms |
   | 200 animals | 74.74 ms | 78.16 ms | 81.62 ms |
   | Reviewer, 0 animals | 79.63 ms | | |
   | Reviewer, 200 animals | 85.29 ms | | |
   | Review-fix head, 0 animals | BENCH0 |
   | Review-fix head, 200 animals | BENCH200 |

   - The `"fauna"` phase alone averages 0.325 ms per tick (0.382 ms in the reviewer's run;
     BENCHPHASE ms at the review-fix head).
   - The budget cited is the foundation audit's (§1): under 167 ms per tick keeps up at ×3.
     Both runs are within it.
   - The 16 ms one-frame figure is already exceeded by the animal-free baseline at 1000
     colonists. Animals add no measurable cost (the difference is noise).
5. **Deterministic `state_hash()`, identical save/load and replay.**
   - `test_fauna_persistence.gd`: the same seed gives the same hash; a mid-run save/load
     continues to the same hash; a replay is ok; a real save file keeps `fauna.rng` bit-exact
     and the run continues identically; an unknown species is refused.
   - `test_fauna_startup.gd`: mapgen counts seed animals through `WorldState.new()`.
   - `test_fauna_species.gd`: the world simulation RNG state is identical with and without
     animals, and the same seed gives the same worldgen placement.
6. **Content rejects bad input.** `test_fauna_species.gd` covers an unknown tile, a duplicate
   id, unknown prey, a mixed diet, and a bad or negative or unknown `animal_counts` entry.
   `test_fauna_startup.gd` shows the same rejection aborts the real `WorldState` startup with
   the typed error.

## Pending owner decisions (non-blocking; listed here instead of blocking)

1. **The animal roster is separate.** The backlog says "actors in the actor table". The animals
   are actor records (`ActorTable.spawn_animal()`, `kind: "animal"`), but they live in
   `FaunaSystem`'s roster, not in `ActorTable`'s colonist or actor lists. This keeps labours,
   needs, jobs, combat and incidents from ever iterating over them, and keeps their A14 cost
   at zero.
2. **How the A14 budget is read.** I cite 167 ms (×3) as the budget; the 1000-colonist baseline
   is already about 75 ms, above 16 ms.
3. **Shipped mapgen seeds no animals** (`animal_counts` is absent). Worldgen places animals only
   when counts are given. Setting default counts is a balance call for the owner.
4. **Logistic births**, with probability `1 − count / cap`, rather than a hard cap
   (ADR 070, Alternatives).
5. **A salted, separate RNG** (`world seed + 0x0FA0A`). It is persisted in `fauna.rng`.
6. **The kind is `"animal"`**, separate from the incident `"wolf"` kind, so raids are unchanged.
7. **The schema is v14**, not the v15 in the owner note: main is at v13 and nothing else
   claimed v14. This needs renumbering if another branch lands v14 first.
8. **Rabbits eat from `crop_tiles` without damaging them.** The damage is F6b-2.
9. **Tests use private access.** They reach `world._fauna` (and `TestAccess` for colonists) to
   place animals, because shipped mapgen seeds none.
10. **The bench lives in the handoff folder**, as an evidence script, not as a `bench_*.gd` in
    `game/scripts/tests/`.
11. **Placeholder dots** are drawn for species without pack art; only the wolf has a sprite.

## Known limitations

- `world_state.gd` stays at 767 lines by putting the new wiring on existing lines. The
  `FaunaSystem.new(...)` call and `_fauna.hash_into()` are chained with `;`. If that style is
  unwelcome, the alternative is to grow the file by about 3 lines, or to move the construction
  into an existing builder.
- `health` and `attack` are stored and persisted, but nothing uses them yet (F6b-3).
- Animals never path-find. A hungry predator takes one greedy step toward the nearest prey in
  sight; other animals wander. This is enough for the acceptance cases, and F6b-3 may replace
  it.
- The owner confirms on the native build after merge (`tools/play.cmd`) by adding
  `animal_counts` to see animals. Animals are not visible with shipped content.
