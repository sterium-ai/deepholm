# L2 handoff: `Vector2i` → `Vector3i` sweep, `layer_count = 1`, save schema v8

> **In short:** Every map tile in `game/scripts/core` is now a `Vector3i(x, y, z)` (ADR 045
> §1, §2, §5 and §6).
>
> - `TileGrid` stores `layer_count`. It owns the flat index across layers,
>   `z * width * height + y * width + x`, so layer 0 keeps its row-major indices.
> - Saves carry `z` beside every `x`/`y` and a `map.layerCount`, with **schema v8**. The v7 → v8
>   migration adds `layerCount 1` and `z 0`, and `save_schema_v8_fixture.json` holds the
>   committed same-session fixture.
> - At the command boundary a missing `z` is the surface. An out-of-range `z` is rejected as
>   `invalid_target`, the same as an out-of-map `x`/`y`.
> - Behaviour does not change. The A2, A14, A16 and R10 scenario dumps match `main` once the
>   added `z`/`layerCount`/`schemaVersion` fields are taken out. Only hash values differ, and
>   they differ by construction.
> - The pre-L2 shape of the same `state_hash()` snapshots still hashes to the old pinned
>   literals. The recorded route searches reproduce their recorded hashes unchanged.
> - Suite: `run_tests: all 132 test(s) PASS  [751s]` (`main` at `c153bd3`: `run_tests: all 131 test(s) PASS  [802s]`).
> - Implementation choices ADR 045 left open are recorded in **ADR 063** (proposed; owner
>   sign-off pending).

## What changed

### `core/map/tile_grid.gd`: the owner of tiles

- `layer_count` (1; it never shrinks). `layer_size()`. `SURFACE := 0`. `MAX_LAYER_COUNT := 64`
  (ADR 063 §4).
- Index helpers:
  - `index_of(Vector3i)` and `coord_of(int)`, with the static `index_in()`/`coord_in()` for
    callers that hold a size and not a grid;
  - `contains(Vector3i)` (`0 <= z < layer_count`), `bounds_max()` and `tile_at(Vector3i)`;
  - `flat_index()` and `flat_coord()` stay planar (an index inside one layer or one rectangle).
- Record helpers: `tile_of(record)` and `write_tile(record, tile)`. The flat `x`/`y`/`z` of
  actors, items, zones, benches and tool locations is read and written only through them.
- String keys:
  - `coord_key()`, `target_key()`, `cell_key()` and `comma_key()` take a `Vector3i`;
  - a surface tile keeps the L1 format byte for byte; a deeper tile appends `_z` or `,z`;
  - inverses: `coord_of_key()` and the new `coord_of_comma_key()`.
- `manhattan`, `chebyshev` and `distance_sq` take `Vector3i` and assert the same layer. They
  stay three formulas.
- Other `Vector3i` APIs:
  - `mark_dirty(Vector3i)`; the dirty-cell log is now `Array[Vector3i]` (§6);
  - the water cache covers every layer;
  - `on_passability_changed(Vector3i)` and `get_room_at(Vector3i)` (regions and rooms cover
    the surface only, ADR 063 §3).
- Hash: `layer_count` is added to `hash_into()`.

### The core sweep (`git grep -c Vector2i game/scripts/core`: 534 on `main`, 11 on the branch)

- **Map:**
  - `Passability` sizes its cache by every layer, and its `is_passable()` and
    `passability()` take a `Vector3i`;
  - `RegionMap.region_of()`/`same_region()`/`reachable()` answer "no region" off the surface;
  - `RouteSearch` has `Vector3i` bounds, keeps its planar neighbours on the current layer, and
    adds the z bound to `_in_bounds()`;
  - the hot loop is unchanged.
- **Records:** actors, ground items, zones, workbenches, tool locations, area orders,
  `pending_dig_finds`, combat targets and `skipped`/`sites` event entries all carry `z`.
  `ActorTable.spawn()`, `ItemTable.place_new_ground_item()`/`spawn_item()`/
  `stage_dig_find()`, `ToolItemStore.spawn_ground()`/`set_ground()`/`set_stockpile()`,
  `ZoneTable.add()`/`overlaps()`/`is_in_any_zone()` and
  `ObjectTable.set_object()`/`get_object()`/… take a `Vector3i`.
- **Callables:** every internal Callable that named a tile with `(x, y)` now takes a
  `Vector3i`. WorldState wires the module methods: `_passability.passability`,
  `_tile_grid.tile_at`, `_tool_store.set_ground`, `_object_table.get_object`, …
- **WorldState's public `(x, y)` queries** keep their signatures and address the surface
  (ADR 063 §2): `get_tile`, `get_object`, `passability`, `is_cell_free`, …. They now call
  `Vector3i(x, y, TileGrid.SURFACE)`.
  - `world_state.gd` is 767 → 767 lines.
  - `_cell_free(Vector3i)` is one new line. `_route_search_factory()` lost its local
    `bounds_max` line (it now uses `TileGrid.bounds_max()`).
- **Command boundary:** `CommandChecks.check_command_layer()`, run by
  `CommandRouter.validate_command()` after the tick check:
  - it fills a missing `z` with the surface on any payload, or `build_line` tile, that has
    both `x` and `y`;
  - a non-int `z` is `invalid_payload` (`payload_value_type`);
  - an out-of-range `z` is `invalid_target` (`target_outside_map`, the out-of-map detail, so
    no new text key);
  - preview and apply share it;
  - the `build`, `cancel_site`, `area_order` and `craft_*` key whitelists accept `z`.
- **Surface-only generators:** world generation, incident edge spawns and scenario steps
  build surface tiles (`TileGrid.SURFACE`).
- **Same-layer searches:** the tool-drop cell search and the flee, rescue and haul cell
  searches stay on the actor's or zone's layer.

### Persistence (schema v8)

- **`StateCodec`:** `SCHEMA_VERSION := 8`. `_encode_tile()` is `{x, y, z}`. The flat records
  have `z`. `map.layerCount` is written beside `width`/`height`, and `decode()` sets
  `layer_count` before rebuilding the dimension services.
- **Validators:** every validator takes `layer_count`. `SaveCheck._valid_tile()` requires and
  bounds `z`, and `_valid_layer()` does the same for the flat records. `MapValidator` requires
  `layerCount` (1..64) and `tiles.size() == width × height × layerCount`.
- **`SaveWire`:**
  - a packed route stays planar on its start's layer, and unpacked tiles take the start's
    `z`;
  - a pre-v8 `{x, y}` route unpacks as it was and the migration adds `z`;
  - `tileRuns` allocation is bounded by `width × height × layerCount` (≤ 64 layers).
- **`SaveMigrations._v7_to_v8()`:** `map.layerCount = 1`, and `z: 0` beside every integer
  `x`/`y` pair. In v7 those are exactly the tiles and flat x/y records, as listed in
  `game-state.schema.json`. The migration never mutates its argument, and refuses a state
  that already has `layerCount`.
- **Fixture:** `game/scripts/tests/fixtures/save_schema_v8_fixture.json`, the R14…F2 session
  written by `docs/orca/handoffs/L2-write-save.gd`.

### Viewer and `boot.gd`

The viewer stays planar until L4 (ADR 045 §7). It reads job targets, site origins, dirty cells
and designations as `Vector3i` and draws the surface ones. Deeper cells are consumed but not
drawn. Its commands send `z = TileGrid.SURFACE`. Files changed: `colonist_panel.gd`,
`designation_overlay.gd`, `map_view.gd`, `order_input.gd` and `workbench_panel.gd`. 167
`Vector2i` remain, all screen or planar viewer coordinates.

## Known limitations

- **The `(x, y)` read shorthands mean the surface.** These are WorldState's public queries,
  `TestAccess`'s helpers, the scenario builder's ring matchers, regions/rooms' planar
  callables and the viewer's screen tiles (ADR 063 §2). L3 and L4 give their callers a layer.
- **Regions and rooms cover layer 0 only.** Off the surface `region_of()` is 0, `reachable()`
  is false and there is no room. `RouteSearch` never leaves its start's layer. Per-layer maps,
  the region graph and link edges are L3.
- **"Nearest" searches assume one layer.** These are tool matching, combat, need, rescue and
  approach ranking. They use the planar metrics, which now assert `a.z == b.z`, so a
  cross-layer call fails loudly. ADR 045 §4 assigns the cross-layer ranking (route cost) to
  L3.
- **`view_snapshot.gd`** (L4's owned path) still exposes actor `x`/`y` columns without `z`.
- **Out-of-range `z` detail.** It reuses `target_outside_map`, so there is no new text key and
  no `data/text` change.
- **Files outside the literal owned paths:** `docs/architecture/save-system.md`,
  `docs/architecture/warnings-baseline.txt`, `docs/decisions/063-…` and
  `docs/decisions/README.md` (see "Contract impact"). **Owner approval pending.**
- **Hash and integrity values in dumps and saves differ from `main` by construction.**
  Equivalence is shown by the projection, not by equal hashes.
